# Contribution log

A dated record of public community contributions — talks, posts, forum answers, tools,
events. One row per activity, logged the **same day** it happens.

## Why it exists

Community recognition programmes (Fabric Community Super User, Microsoft MVP) are assessed
on a portfolio of contributions from the **last ~12 months**, submitted as roughly a dozen
activities with links and audience figures. Reach and impact count for more than volume.

Reconstructing a year of activity from memory is where most portfolios fall apart: the
meetup page is gone, the view count has moved, nobody remembers how many people were in the
room. Logging the same day costs 60 seconds and is the whole difference.

The other reason: this log makes an honest quarter obvious. Six blank weeks in the table is
information.

## How to use it

- Add the row **the day it happens**, newest at the top.
- **Reach** is the number you would put in front of a reviewer: attendees, views, accepted
  solutions, stars, downloads. Record what you can verify, mark estimates with `~`, and leave
  it blank rather than guessing.
- **Evidence** is a link that will still resolve in a year — a recording, a screenshot in
  `shared/assets/`, an archived event page. Live counters change; capture them at the time.
- Re-check reach numbers on anything significant about a month later and update the row.
- Never log anything that exposes client or employer data.

## Milestones I control

These are the checkpoints worth aiming at, because they do not depend on anyone else's
decision. The award, if it comes, is a lagging indicator of these.

- [ ] Daily habit of answering on the Fabric Community forums and Microsoft Q&A
- [ ] Fabric Community **Super User** status
- [ ] Publishing cadence held for a full quarter without a gap
- [ ] First three talks delivered and recorded
- [ ] A user group started or actively co-organised
- [ ] One open tool with users who are not me
- [ ] PL-300 / DP-600 (they do not count toward the award — they get you on the stage)
- [ ] Product feedback filed publicly on Fabric previews

## Nomination notes

- **You cannot nominate yourself.** The nomination has to come from an existing MVP or a
  Microsoft employee, so the relationships are a months-long dependency, not a final step.
  Confirm the current rules at <https://mvp.microsoft.com> before relying on any of this.
- Power BI sits in the **Data Platform** category, which now leans heavily toward Fabric.
- The main award cycle lands on **1 July**, with quarterly rounds in between. Allow ~90 days
  for review, so a nomination aimed at July 2027 wants to be in by **February–March 2027**.
- Once nominated there is roughly a **45-day** window to submit the portfolio — which is a
  copy-and-paste job if this file has been kept up, and a nightmare if it hasn't.
- Lead with impact, not activity: "180 people attended", not "I ran a session".
